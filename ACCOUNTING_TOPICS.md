# Accounting syllabus: basics to Chartered Accountant level

Topics only. ★ = the part Divine Books covers or will cover.

## Level 1: Basics
- What accounting is; users of accounts; accounting vs bookkeeping
- Accounting concepts and principles (going concern, accrual, matching, prudence, consistency)
- Accounting equation: Assets = Liabilities + Capital
- Types of accounts: personal, real, nominal; golden rules of debit and credit ★
- Source documents: invoice, bill, voucher, receipt, challan, debit/credit note ★
- Journal ★
- Ledger and posting ★
- Cash book, bank book, petty cash ★
- Trial balance ★
- Subsidiary books: sales, purchase, returns books ★
- Rectification of errors; suspense account
- Bank reconciliation statement ★
- Bills of exchange, cheques, promissory notes
- Depreciation methods (straight line, written down value) and fixed assets ★
- Provisions and reserves
- Final accounts: trading account, profit & loss, balance sheet ★
- Adjustments: outstanding, prepaid, accrued, advance, bad debts, closing stock ★
- Capital vs revenue items

## Level 2: Intermediate
- Single entry system; accounts from incomplete records
- Partnership accounts: admission, retirement, death, dissolution, goodwill
- Company accounts: shares, debentures, issue, forfeiture, buy-back
- Not-for-profit accounts: receipts & payments, income & expenditure
- Branch and departmental accounts; consignment; joint ventures
- Hire purchase and instalment; royalty
- Inventory valuation: FIFO, weighted average, net realisable value ★
- Accounting for inventory and cost of goods sold ★
- Bank loans, EMI, interest calculation ★
- Cash flow statement
- Ratio analysis (liquidity, profitability, leverage, turnover)
- Fund flow statement
- Receivables and payables management; ageing ★
- Petty cash imprest system
- Payroll accounting: salary, PF, ESI, professional tax ★
- Accounting standards (Ind AS / AS) overview

## Level 3: Cost and management accounting
- Cost concepts and classification; cost sheet
- Material, labour, overhead accounting; absorption, activity-based costing
- Job costing ★ (per-job profit for CCTV and wiring work)
- Process costing; contract costing ★
- Marginal costing; break-even analysis
- Standard costing and variance analysis
- Budgeting and budgetary control
- Working capital management
- Capital budgeting (NPV, IRR, payback)

## Level 4: Taxation (India)
- GST: registration, supply, place of supply, time of supply, value of supply ★
- GST rates, HSN/SAC, input tax credit and its conditions, blocked credits ★
- Reverse charge (RCM), composition scheme, QRMP scheme ★
- GST invoices, credit/debit notes, e-invoice, e-way bill ★
- GST returns: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, 2A/2B reconciliation, 9/9C annual ★
- GST audit, refunds, assessments, appeals
- Income tax: heads of income, deductions (80C etc.), advance tax, ITR forms
- Business income: presumptive taxation (44AD, 44ADA), books and audit limits, depreciation under tax
- Capital gains; set-off and carry forward of losses
- TDS and TCS: sections, rates, deposit, quarterly returns, certificates
- Professional tax; labour laws: PF, ESI, gratuity, bonus
- MSME rules (45-day payment rule)

## Level 5: Auditing and law
- Audit concepts: statutory, internal, tax audit, GST audit
- Audit evidence, sampling, internal control and checks ★
- Vouching and verification ★
- Companies Act: books of accounts, financial statements, schedule III, board reports
- LLP and partnership law; sole proprietorship compliance
- Auditor's reports, CARO, reporting standards
- Fraud, ethics, professional conduct

## Level 6: Advanced financial accounting (CA Final)
- Consolidated financial statements; group accounts
- Business combinations, mergers, amalgamations, demergers
- Foreign currency transactions (AS 11 / Ind AS 21)
- Financial instruments (Ind AS 32, 107, 109)
- Revenue recognition (Ind AS 115); leases (Ind AS 116)
- Deferred tax; impairment; provisions and contingencies
- Segment, interim and related-party reporting
- Earnings per share
- Valuation of shares and business; goodwill
- Insolvency and liquidation accounting

## Level 7: Strategic finance and control
- Financial management: cost of capital, capital structure, dividend policy
- Derivatives, risk management, treasury
- Mergers and acquisitions finance; business valuation
- Financial modelling, forecasting, MIS reporting
- ERP and accounting information systems; data analytics; controls ★
- Internal financial controls, risk management, governance

## What a small business needs from this list
Level 1 fully, Level 2 selected (inventory, ageing, payroll, bank reconciliation), Level 3 job costing, Level 4 GST and TDS basics, and Level 5 vouching and internal control. The rest is for your CA.
